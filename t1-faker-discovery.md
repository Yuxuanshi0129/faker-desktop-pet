# T1 Faker Mascot Discovery

## Identity and category

T1 is a global esports organization with a League of Legends team. The intended subject is inferred to be Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, T1's current League of Legends mid laner. This interpretation is strongly supported by the official LoL Esports roster and Riot's Hall of Legends profile.

## Audience and character direction

Design for League of Legends and esports fans, including viewers who recognize Faker from competitive play. Translate his public image as a composed, technically exceptional playmaker and in-game leader into a focused but approachable desktop companion. The chibi direction below is an interpretation for the pet format, not an official T1 mascot design.

## Visual language

- Palette: black and vivid red as the main esports cues, with white or cool light gray for contrast. Keep colors clean and high contrast.
- Shape language: compact, rounded chibi proportions; neat silhouette; restrained geometric accents that suggest precision and stage lighting.
- Human cues: short black hair, round glasses, calm confident expression, and a simple black-and-red esports jersey.
- Optional motifs: a subtle mid-lane line or a small controlled red energy accent can suggest decisive gameplay. Keep these secondary to the recognizable human character.

## Avoid

Avoid readable T1, sponsor, game, or manufacturer logos and text; unauthorized exact jersey replicas; aggressive demon horns, fangs, or frightening expressions; excessive flames, weapons, trophies, or visual clutter; and implying an official mascot or endorsement. The "Unkillable Demon King" is a documented nickname, but literal demon styling is optional and should remain understated.

## Evidence and confidence

- High confidence: T1 team identity; Faker's real name and current T1 roster membership; Faker's mid-lane role. Sources 1-3.
- High confidence: Riot describes Faker as T1's mid laner, recognized for technical skill, game-changing plays, and in-game leadership. Source 2.
- Medium confidence / inferred design guidance: black-red-white palette, glasses, jersey silhouette, chibi proportions, and subtle lane or energy motifs. These are practical visual interpretations for a pet, not claims about official character art or mascot canon.
- Current roster evidence is from the live LoL Esports team page, checked 2026-10-03. League roles can change; recheck before future reuse.
